After the sanzen the Roshi returns to the Zendo.

When he sits down:

1 inkin bell – 2 clappers <i class="gloss">(taku)</i> Wait a moment:

1 inkin bell

Then the following things happen at the same time:

1. Jikijitsu stands up to light a candle and incense stick. He puts out the mat.

2. HAN

3. Jisha brings water to the altar at the end of HAN.

3 bells
