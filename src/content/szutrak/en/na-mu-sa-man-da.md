### Eko

We humbly request the Three Treasures to lead us to true wisdom. We dedicate the merit of reciting the Heart Sutra to Shakyamuni Butsu and celebrate his supreme enlightment. May we fulfill the obligations to the Four Benefactors, benefit all living things throughout the Three Realms, and help all beings attain perfect wisdom.

⟦O⟧

All Buddhas throughout space and time, all Bodhisattvas,

⟦O⟧

Mahasattvas, the great Prajna ⟦O⟧

Paramita.
